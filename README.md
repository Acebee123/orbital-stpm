# Orbital — STPM Chemistry Topic 3

Premium React Three Fiber / Three.js learning flow: Atomic Orbitals → Hybridisation → Hybrid orbital arrangement.

Run `pnpm install`, then `pnpm dev`. Build with `pnpm build`; verify scientific and interaction invariants with `pnpm test`.

Open `orbital.html` for the standalone browser version. It contains the production JavaScript, CSS, 3D scenes and generated sounds. No runtime installation is required. Google Fonts is optional, with system fonts offline. WebGL must be available. Recreate the HTML with `pnpm export:html` or `node scripts/export-html.mjs` after installing dependencies.

## Atomic Orbitals

The eleven selections are 1s / 2s / 3s, 2px / 2py / 2pz and all five 3d orbitals. Each shape is built from 56,000 dense probability samples, visible at normal distance and throughout zoom. There are no orbital surfaces, outlines, nuclei or nuclear zoom stages in this module. Amber and teal encode opposite wavefunction phases, not charge. Sample positions are fixed; only their soft intensity shimmers. Shapes never breathe or scale cyclically. p and d selection transitions crossfade smoothly over 1.25 seconds. s selections immediately remove the outgoing cloud and softly reveal only the newly selected distribution; two different s orbitals are never superimposed, including during rapid selection. The 1s, 2s and 3s clouds use increasing illustrative radii (1.35, 2.15 and 3.05 display units) on the same viewing scale, with corresponding radial nodes and phases scaled consistently. These are qualitative sizes, not hard physical boundaries.

One scientific coordinate frame puts z upright. dz² has two vertical z lobes and an equatorial ring in xy; both the cloud and labelled axes share this frame. All angular and radial nodes are preserved by the hydrogen-like probability kernel. A qualitative envelope weights the distribution toward the familiar orbital shapes, with a faint short tail. Samples are probability-density samples, not individual electrons or trajectories. This is an educational representation, not a numerical many-electron quantum calculation. Display dimensions are adjusted for clarity; the intensity shimmer does not imply a time-varying stationary-state probability density.

Drag, scroll or pinch to inspect. Overview, Closer and the zoom slider smoothly dolly while preserving the viewing direction. Reset camera restores the recommended view. Labels, Axes, radial density cutaways for 2s/3s, highlight selection and Motion remain available. Cutaways are off by default.

## Hybridisation

Only one s and one, two or three p orbitals enter sp, sp² and sp³. There are no molecular examples, hydrogen partners, nuclei or sigma bonds in this section. Source orbitals reuse the Atomic Orbitals probability sampler. All starting, intermediate and final orbitals are rendered exclusively as dense points, with no meshes, shells or orbital outlines. Each hybrid has 112,000 fine probability samples; phase colours remain amber and teal. Sample intensity shimmers gently without particle trajectories.

The independent 16-second teaching timeline holds the source orbitals in a clean, centred row for three seconds, moves them inward, pauses before mixing, then continuously redistributes and reorients their probability samples over six seconds using a GPU morph. Final samples follow the squared linear combination of hydrogen-like 2s and direction-aligned 2p wavefunctions, with exact 1/2, 1/3 or 1/4 s character and a sculpted qualitative shape envelope. Forward lobes taper from a shared origin into an elongated body; narrow reverse lobes are orbital phase regions, not atoms. Axial slices are stratified, with a smooth density gradient to zero at the edge and no samples outside the fitted support. Reverse-lobe visibility and display sizes are adjusted for teaching rather than quantitative populations. Smaller Gaussian points, density-weighted size/brightness and restrained shimmer create a continuous-looking cloud. Four seconds remain to inspect the final equivalent orbitals: linear 180°, trigonal planar 120°, tetrahedral 109.5°. The morph represents linear combinations of wavefunctions; it is not a literal reaction or excitation. Replay, next steps, seeking and speed operate on this hybrid-only timeline.

Camera orbit and scroll/pinch zoom stay enabled during the hybridisation animation. Input immediately yields cinematic guidance without pausing the chemistry. The user's angle survives release, pause/resume, next steps, timeline navigation, replay and hybrid selection. Only Reset View explicitly restores the teaching angle through damping. Playback pauses when the browser tab is hidden.

Sound uses the native Web Audio API. The original noise and oscillator sources, filters and pitch sweeps remain. Separate whoosh, impact and energy GainNodes feed a master GainNode and DynamicsCompressorNode. Sound Volume ramps master gain without clicks. Sound On/Off is separate and gesture-activated. Hybridisation uses movement and settling cues without bond-contact effects.
